TALESPIRE VR - CONTROLS
=======================

Start SteamVR first, then start TaleSpire. The buttons below are for Valve Index controllers;
other controllers are listed under OTHER CONTROLLERS.

VR MODE OR NORMAL MODE
----------------------
On the black bar along the bottom of TaleSpire's main menu, between the "Welcome" line (under
Begin) and the Build-id in the bottom right corner, is a "VR MODE" button: click it to
switch between VR and normal TaleSpire (the headset, or your monitor with mouse and keyboard).
Your choice is saved: TaleSpire starts in that mode next time. In normal mode TaleSpire runs
exactly as it does without the mod - that button is the only thing added. Switching to VR needs
SteamVR running (if it isn't, the button says so and stays in normal mode).
If VR ever won't start and you can't reach the button, open VRMod\TaleSpireVR.Core.ini in a text
editor and set VR = false (or true) under [Mode].

SETTINGS FILES (all directly in the VRMod folder, created the first time you start TaleSpire with
the mod; each option is explained in the file. Edit them while TaleSpire is closed: they are read
when it starts.)
  TaleSpireVR.Core.ini .......... VR or normal mode at startup, render resolution, desktop window
  TaleSpireVR.Controls.ini ...... the table view: starting scale, moving, zooming, turning, laser
  TaleSpireVR.Menus.ini ......... the floating menus, the radial menu, the forearm dice
  TaleSpireVR.Graphics.ini ...... draw distance, shadows, bloom, anti-aliasing
  TaleSpireVR.StreamCamera.ini .. the stream window and held camera, or turn them off (STREAM VIEW)
  TaleSpireVR.FirstPerson.ini ... first-person mode, hands and weapons
What you pick in game for each creature (not settings): BepInEx\config\TaleSpireVR holds
TaleSpireVR.Weapons.txt and TaleSpireVR.HandColors.txt.

PLAYING TOGETHER
----------------
The mod works for the Game Master and for players. Everyone plays on their own TaleSpire through
TaleSpire's own online play; nobody else needs the mod, and the mod itself never sends anything over
the internet. What you do in VR - moving minis, attacks, dice, pings, rulers, first person walking -
is done with TaleSpire's own actions, so every player sees it as usual. What only you (and your
stream window) see: your hands and their colours, weapons and their flashes and sounds, the flame
and ice powers, the dice results and turn order floating in the sky, the faster attack timing.
As a player (not the Game Master) TaleSpire's rules for players apply, and also:
  - Build Mode and the Build Grid are greyed out on the button row under the menu panel.
  - First person, hand colours and weapons only for creatures TaleSpire lets you control.
  - No Predator Vision, and no stream window or held camera.
  - Cutscenes: when the Game Master shows one, it plays on a screen the size of the menu panel in
    front of you, with "The Game Master is showing a cutscene" above it.

POINTING
--------
Both controllers are laser pointers. Pull the trigger on whichever controller is pointing at
the thing you want - that controller takes control.
  Blue laser   = pointing at a menu.  A yellow ring on the menu shows exactly where you'll click.
  Yellow laser = pointing at the board (tiles, props, creatures).
A small dot marks where each laser touches something.
Which laser is in control:
  - Only pressing a button changes it: the trigger you pull, and A or the stick click always
    use the RIGHT laser (they are on the right controller).
  - Resting the other laser on the menu lights up the menu buttons under it, but never takes
    the board away from the hand you are using (its trigger on the board still picks up).
  - Whatever you carry or are placing (a creature, a group, a mini / prop / tile / slab taken
    from a menu) stays with the hand that took it until you drop or place it; the other
    hand's trigger on the board does nothing meanwhile.
  - When you switch hands, or press on the board while the other laser rests on the menu, the
    press waits about 1/10 of a second so TaleSpire can see what the laser points at
    (otherwise the pickup would miss).


RIGHT CONTROLLER
----------------
Trigger ............ Left mouse click.
                     - On a menu: click buttons, lists, sliders (hold + move to drag/scroll).
                     - On the board: click to select; HOLD on a creature to pick it up,
                       move the laser to carry it, RELEASE to drop it (it falls to the
                       floor below, just like the normal game). A held creature stays
                       still and keeps facing the same way until you move or turn it.
                     - In Build Mode: place / drag tiles and props.
A .................. Right mouse click.
                     - On a creature, door or other object: opens its radial menu. The menu
                       appears as a floating window in front of you; point at an icon (it
                       grows and shows its name) and pull the trigger.
                     - With a radial menu open: A on another creature / object opens that
                       one's menu instead; A on the same one, on the ground, or on the radial
                       menu itself closes it.
                     - Elsewhere: cancel / back out of the current tool.
                     - The floating radial window only catches your laser on the menu itself
                       (its ring of options with a small margin, its option lists, our extra
                       buttons, and while one is open the whole hand-colour or weapons arc, or
                       the Weapon Catalogue); just outside it your laser goes through to the
                       board again.
                     - A creature's menu has one extra teal button (an eye) ABOVE its ring of
                       options: "First Person" - see through that creature's eyes (FIRST-PERSON
                       MODE below). TaleSpire's own options stay where they always are.
                     - A UNIQUE creature's menu also has a blue button with a hand: "Hand Colour"
                       (HAND COLOURS under FIRST-PERSON MODE), and a red button with a crossed
                       sword and staff: "Weapons" (WEAPONS under FIRST-PERSON MODE).
                     Holding A never drags the board around (TaleSpire's right-drag camera
                     move is off in VR - move with the sticks).
                     A always works at once: a second A right after the first (closing a menu
                     you just opened, or opening the next one) is never swallowed.
B .................. Show / hide the big menu panel (it appears in front of you). With a radial
                     menu open, B closes the radial menu and opens the panel. Opening a radial
                     menu while the panel is up closes the panel (see MENUS below).
                     On a board, opening it also opens TaleSpire's toolbars (what Space
                     does: dice, hotbar, side menus) if they are closed.
                     On the title screen and campaign/board lists the panel is always
                     shown; pressing B brings it back in front of you.
                     In first person: TaleSpire's ESC menu instead, on its own (see FIRST-PERSON
                     MODE > ESC MENU).
Thumbstick left/right  Turn 30 degrees (snap turn).
                     While HOLDING a creature: turns the creature left/right instead
                     (like Alt + mouse in the normal game).
                     In first person: the same snap turn.
Thumbstick up/down . Move your viewpoint UP / DOWN (same as Page Up / Page Down). Keep
                     pushing down to reach the ground or dip below it (up to about 5 m).
                     In first person: UP = jump, DOWN = crouch / stand; flying creatures fly up /
                     down (see FIRST-PERSON MODE).
                     While your laser is on the menu panel it SCROLLS the menu under the
                     laser instead (lists, the library, side menus like Atmosphere).
Thumbstick left/right  (On the menu panel: scrolls sideways lists such as the library grids -
                     right = onward. Snap turning is off while your laser is on the menu.)
Thumbstick click ... Middle mouse click (rotates the held tile/prop 90 degrees in Build Mode).
                     While the stream view is HELD: start / stop its ORBIT around the point
                     your right laser is on (see STREAM VIEW below).
Trackpad press ..... Rotate modifier (same as holding Alt): while carrying a creature, a
  (hold)             group or a prop, press and point - it turns to face where you point.
                     While you carry a creature or a group, only the trackpads (and the zoom
                     buttons) switch it between moving, turning and raising: a stray Alt / Ctrl
                     on the real keyboard can no longer leave it stuck turning.
                     Both trackpads together for 1 second: hold / release the stream view
                     (see STREAM VIEW below; Game Master only).
                     In first person: both trackpads held = PREDATOR VISION (see FIRST-PERSON MODE;
                     Game Master only).
Trackpad TAP ....... PING (TaleSpire's T): a quick press and release puts the animated red
                     arrows where this laser points on the board, for everyone to see (on a
                     creature they follow it). Drawn twice TaleSpire's size in VR.
Grip + Trigger ..... LASSO: squeeze the grip firmly (laser and its dots turn purple), then pull and
                     hold the trigger and sweep the laser around creatures on the board.
                     Let go of the trigger - every creature inside the loop is selected.
                     Pull the trigger alone on any selected creature to pick up the whole
                     group; it moves together, without turning. Turn the group with the
                     thumbstick (it turns around the creature you grabbed, keeping its
                     formation); B / A on the left controller raise and lower it.
                     Pull the trigger on empty board to deselect.


LEFT CONTROLLER
---------------
Thumbstick ......... Move over the board (forward/back/left/right, relative to where
                     you are looking). Zoomed in: always the starting speed. Zoomed out:
                     faster the further out you are, so big maps stay quick to cross.
Thumbstick click ... Toggle Build Mode. It always works: a slab, tile or prop still on your
                     pointer is put down first (as A would), and a text box you were typing in
                     (e.g. the library search) stops typing. (TaleSpire's own B key does
                     nothing in either case, so the mod switches Build Mode directly.)
                     In first person: show / hide the ruler instead.
B (upper button) ... Zoom IN  - the board grows around the spot you are looking at.
                     While HOLDING a creature: raises the creature instead
                     (like Ctrl + mouse in the normal game).
A (lower button) ... Zoom OUT - the board shrinks around the spot you are looking at.
                     While HOLDING a creature: lowers the creature instead.
Trigger ............ Left mouse click with the LEFT hand's laser.
Trackpad press ..... Elevate modifier (same as holding Ctrl): while carrying a creature or
  (hold)             prop, press and move the laser up/down to raise/lower it.
                     Both trackpads together for 1 second: hold / release the stream view
                     (Game Master only).
Trackpad TAP ....... PING where the LEFT laser points (same as the right trackpad tap).
Palm up ............ Turn the left wrist so the palm faces up (wherever you are looking): the DICE
                     appear on your inner forearm (see DICE below).
Grip + Trigger ..... MEASURE: squeeze the grip firmly (laser and its dots turn green), point where
                     the measurement starts, pull and hold the trigger, move the laser,
                     and let go of the trigger where it ends.
                     Uses the ruler type you last picked in TaleSpire's ruler menu (open
                     the menu with B once to choose line / circle / cone / box).
                     - A cone needs its width too: after letting go, point and pull the
                       trigger once more to set it.
                     - The distance is shown as a label in the world next to the ruler.
                     - The ruler stays up so you can read it, until your next press on the
                       board: that press puts the ruler away and then works as usual (picks
                       up the creature, opens the door...). A quick grip + trigger pull
                       (without moving) also clears it.

Grip + trigger only measures / lassos when the laser is on the board; on a menu it is a
normal click. The grip on its own does nothing, and only a firm squeeze counts, so your
normal hold on the controllers won't set it off. One squeeze = one measurement or lasso: to do
another, let go of the grip and squeeze again. Until then the trigger is a normal click, even if
you keep gripping firmly (the laser is only green / purple while a squeeze is ready to use).
While carrying a creature the grips are ignored.


DICE
----
OPEN ........... Turn your LEFT wrist counter-clockwise, palm towards up (about 35 degrees past the
                 usual grip with palms facing each other - a bit over a third of the way to fully palm up;
                 it hides again once you turn back to under about 17 degrees): TaleSpire's dice row appears
                 on the inside of your forearm, lying along it (towards the wrist), as if strapped to it.
                 It depends only on how the hand is turned around the forearm - not on where you look,
                 nor on whether the forearm points level, up or down - and the palm has to
                 stay up for a moment, so passing through while you point, place things or use menus
                 doesn't show it. Turn the hand back and it disappears (it stays while the right laser
                 is on it). Only on a board, not in first person, not while a radial menu is open or the
                 left trigger is held. While it is out, the left laser is switched off (with your palm
                 up it would point sideways across your view). Its size: DiceMenuWidth in
                 TaleSpireVR.Menus.ini. (The dice in the big menu panel work the same way.)
PICK DICE ...... Keep your left palm up and point the RIGHT laser at a die (d4 ... d100), pull the
                 trigger, as in the normal game: each click adds one to the tray that rises above the row.
TAKE THEM ...... Pull the trigger on the tray (the box holding your dice), or on its small GM button
                 for a hidden GM roll. A click is enough: the dice appear right by your hand, a
                 little way out along your pointer (about 15 cm), and stay there as you move it; the
                 pointer is short while you hold them, ending at the dice. (Holding the trigger on the
                 tray and dragging off it works too, like the normal game; the forearm dice and the
                 dice in the big menu work the same.) The forearm dice and the big menu step aside
                 while you hold dice; the forearm dice come back after the throw if your palm is still
                 turned up.
THROW .......... Pull and hold the trigger, swing your hand as if throwing real dice onto the board
                 and let go: they fly off from your hand the way, and as fast as, they were moving
                 (a still hand just drops them).
CLEAN-UP ....... Your thrown dice leave the board by themselves 10 seconds after they land (TaleSpire's
                 "Remove", so they are gone for everyone). Pick them up again (or throw them again)
                 before that and the 10 seconds start over when they land again. Other players' dice
                 are left alone.
RESULTS ........ The result (who rolled, the dice, the total) floats in the sky in the direction you
                 face, a little above the horizon: look slightly up to read it. It is the same size
                 whatever your zoom, stays clear of the board below, shows on the stream view for your
                 players too, and stays up 15 seconds (5 in the normal game). Only your own view
                 changes: other players see TaleSpire's usual result, and the roll itself is
                 TaleSpire's.
TURNS .......... In turn-based mode, whenever the turn changes (or the lineup does), TaleSpire's own
                 turn order bar (the portraits at the top of the normal game's screen) floats in the
                 same place in the sky for 9 seconds, with whose turn it is above it ("Your turn"
                 when it is one of your creatures', as a player). Messages such as "Not Goblin's
                 turn" show there too. The turns are TaleSpire's; only where they are shown is ours.
Dice are not available in first person.


MENUS - HOW THEY FIT TOGETHER
-----------------------------
One menu at a time; the newest one wins, and closing a menu never opens another one:
  - Opening a radial menu (A on a creature / object) while the big menu panel is up closes the panel.
    Closing the radial does not bring the panel back - press B for it.
  - B while a radial menu is open closes the radial and opens the panel. B again closes the panel - and
    TaleSpire's ESC menu or settings window if you opened one in it (they don't stay floating on their own).
  - The dice results in the sky are hidden while TaleSpire's ESC menu is open.
  - A with a radial menu open: on another creature / object = that one's menu; on the same one,
    the ground, or the radial menu itself = it closes.
  - The hand-colour and weapons arcs are part of the radial menu they open from: they close with it, and
    A on them closes the whole menu like A anywhere on a radial menu.
  - The Weapon Catalogue (+ on the weapons arc) takes the radial menu's place: Return brings the menu back
    with its weapons arc; A closes the catalogue and the menu together.
  - The forearm dice are not a menu you open: they show while your left palm is turned up (not while a
    radial menu is open). Dice in hand: the panel and the forearm dice step aside until the throw.
  - In first person there is no panel: B is TaleSpire's ESC menu on its own, and A follows the first-person
    rules (FIRST-PERSON MODE below). The same rules apply: a radial menu opening closes the ESC menu, B
    with a radial menu open closes the radial and opens the ESC menu, and A or B closes the ESC menu
    without opening anything else.


VR KEYBOARD
-----------
Click any text box in the menus (library search, filters, names, join ID): a keyboard appears
under the menu panel. Point at keys and pull the trigger - with either hand, or both hands
taking turns like two fingers (each laser lights up its own key).
  Shift  - next letter uppercase / symbol      Back  - delete (hold to repeat)
  Clear  - empty the box                       Enter - run the search / submit, and close
  Done   - close the keyboard
It also closes when you click anything that is not a text box, or close the menu with B.
Your physical keyboard keeps working too.


FLOOR SLIDER (right edge of the big menu panel, on a board)
-----------------------------------------------------------
The thin vertical slider with pins: point at a pin, hold the trigger and drag up/down. The pins
move at a third of your laser's speed so small changes are easy (FloorSliderSpeed in TaleSpireVR.Menus.ini).
  Green pin (top by default) - view level / cut plane: hides everything above it.
  Blue pin                   - water level.
  Fog pin                    - fog level (only on boards that have fog).
The camera-height pin is hidden: the right thumbstick does that in VR.
(The slider is drawn bigger in VR; that enlargement holds still while you drag a pin, so the pin
follows your laser smoothly however many menus you have opened and closed.)


STREAM VIEW (for players watching on Discord / recording with OBS)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
A second window, "TaleSpire VR - Stream View", opens with the game. Share THAT window on
Discord (or add it to OBS as a Window Capture). The picture is 16:9 at your monitor's resolution.
It is a Game Master's tool: while you play as a player the window closes (and opens again when you
are the Game Master). To turn the whole feature off (no window, no held camera), set Enabled = false
under [StreamView] in VRMod\TaleSpireVR.StreamCamera.ini.
On controllers without trackpads, "both trackpads" below means both grips pressed in.
  Normally ....... it shows what you see in the headset (the left eye, like the game's window).
  HOLD ........... press BOTH trackpads together and keep them pressed for 1 second (not while
                   carrying a creature). The stream's viewpoint stays where your head is right
                   now - same framing, horizon kept level - but it stays live: creatures you
                   move, doors, lights and the view-level slider all show up in it. Walk around,
                   zoom, fly, or take the headset off: the players' view does not move.
  RELEASE ........ both trackpads for 1 second again: the stream camera flies smoothly back to
                   your eyes (over a gentle arc, at about half your top stick speed, never longer
                   than 3 seconds), then follows your headset again - no sudden cut for the
                   players. Holding again during the flight holds wherever you are now.
                   Leaving or switching boards releases it at once.
  ORBIT .......... while held, point your RIGHT laser at a spot on the board and click the right
                   thumbstick: the stream camera circles that spot at its current height,
                   slowly (a sixteenth of your top stick speed), always facing it so it stays
                   in the middle of the picture - a moving bird's-eye view of the battle map.
                   The spot is exactly where your laser's dot is: on top of a hill, a slab, a
                   wall or a creature (the camera tilts to keep that height centred); where
                   the laser hits nothing, the ground. A small green ring marks the spot in
                   your headset. Click the thumbstick again to stop (the view stays where it
                   is). To circle another spot: stop, point at the new spot, click again.
                   (While carrying or placing something the click still rotates it instead.)
  Bird's-eye view for the players: move up high (right stick up, or zoom out), look down at
  the board, hold, then come back down close to the minis to play.
  FIRST PERSON ... while you are in first-person mode the stream always shows your first-person
                   view. A held view (and its orbit) waits, paused, and comes back exactly as it
                   was when you leave first person. The two-trackpad hold does nothing meanwhile.

While held you see (in the headset only, never on the stream) a green wireframe movie camera
where the view is held, pointing where it looks, and four green lines along the edges of its
view. They frame a little LESS than the stream really shows: 10% in from each edge of the
picture (top, bottom, left and right), so anything you keep inside them stays clear of whatever
your players' stream windows put around the edges. Each line ends where that framed corner meets
the board's ground floor (level 0 - the dirt layer, the lowest level minis stand on), and a
green outline on that floor marks the framed area. If the view is tilted down only a little, its
top corners see sky, not floor: those two lines end at the stream's draw distance and the
outline's far edge follows it. Your own draw distance cuts the lines like everything else. The
camera icon appears once you move away from the spot where you held it, and keeps a readable size
however far away it is. How far in the frame is drawn: HeldViewFrameInset in
TaleSpireVR.StreamCamera.ini (0.1 = 10%; 0 = the exact edges).

The stream window: double-click it (or press F11 while it is focused) for borderless fullscreen
at full resolution; double-click or Esc to go back. Drag its edges to resize - it stays 16:9.
Don't minimize it (Windows stops drawing minimized windows, so the stream would go black). If
you close it, holding the view again reopens it. To record both views in OBS, add one Window
Capture of the Stream View and one of TaleSpire's own window.

Frame rate: the held view runs at 30 fps (HeldViewFps). Each of its frames draws everything it
sees again from its own viewpoint - even things you are also looking at - so it costs headset
frame rate in proportion to how busy its view is. Light boards don't notice; on a very big board
your headset can lose around a fifth of its frame rate while the view is held (a bit less with
HeldViewFps = 20). Following your headset costs nothing noticeable.
Stream settings (frame rates, field of view, how far the held camera sees, the green marker)
are in TaleSpireVR.StreamCamera.ini.


FIRST-PERSON MODE (see the board through a creature's eyes)
-----------------------------------------------------------
ENTER .......... A on a creature to open its radial menu, then the teal eye button above the ring
                 ("First Person"). The view blinks (darkness slides in from the sky and the ground
                 and back out, one second) and you are standing where the creature stands, at its
                 size: your real eye height becomes the creature's eye height (from its model's
                 height), and you face the way it faces. You have light brown hands that follow
                 your fingers (Valve Index finger tracking: each finger as you hold it; other
                 controllers: the trigger curls the index finger and the grip the other three,
                 and the thumb follows what the hand does - resting on a button or stick, along
                 the fingers when pointing, wrapped in a fist, open while you cast a power).
                 Only creatures TaleSpire lets you control (a Game Master: any; a player: their own).
  OTHERS SEE ... your creature walks with you on everyone's board, live (TaleSpire's own moving-mini
                 display), and is put down for real whenever you stop walking for a second and when
                 you leave. Your attacks start from where you are.
  NOT ITS TURN . in turn-based mode you can be a creature whose turn it isn't and look around, but
                 not walk, jump or attack with it ("Not <name>'s turn" shows in the sky if you try);
                 it can move again as soon as its turn comes. The same during a cutscene.
YOUR CREATURE .. Hidden, but it goes everywhere with you, facing where you look, and it is the
                 selected creature the whole time (you can't select another one until you leave).
                 So its attacks and emotes start where you stand: open another creature's menu
                 (A on it) > Attacks, and the attack flies from you to it.
MOVE ........... Left stick: walk forward / back / sideways (where you look), 2 grid squares per
                 second. Real steps in your play space move you too. Creatures never block you;
                 walls, tiles and props do. You go through doorways and under arches and fall off
                 edges. Small bumps you just walk over; walk into a ledge up to 2 squares high and
                 you climb onto it (a short climb: up first, then over the edge, so your eyes
                 never pass through the floor above).
TURN ........... Right stick left / right: the same turning as outside first person (30 degree snap
                 turns by default; smooth if SmoothTurn is on in TaleSpireVR.Controls.ini).
JUMP ........... Right stick UP (push and let go): a split-second crouch, then a gentle jump that clears
                 about 2.5 squares. Jump while walking into a ledge: once it is within climbing
                 reach you climb onto it and keep walking - so a ledge up to 4.5 squares above your
                 floor (2.5 jump + 2 climb) can be reached.
CROUCH ......... Right stick DOWN: crouch to 2/3 of your height ("sneak"; your view darkens strongly
                 around the edges while you crouch); push down again to stand (only if there is room
                 above).
                 (Jump and crouch are on the right stick because the left stick is busy walking.)
STATUS EFFECTS . Your creature's status effects (Frozen, Burning, Holy, Confused...) play around your
                 invisible body as they would around the mini: ice at your feet, auras around you.
                 Look down or around to see them. The parts that would be right in front of your
                 eyes are cut away so your view ahead stays clear (an effect that surrounds the head
                 is hidden while it surrounds your eyes).
FLYING ......... If the creature is flying (also if you switch Flying on from its menu while you are
                 it), right stick up / down flies up / down instead, with no gravity; switch
                 Flying off and you drop to the floor.
POINTING ....... Your lasers work exactly as always, they are just not drawn: squeeze a grip to see
                 that hand's (white) laser, and both show while any radial menu or the ESC menu (B) is open.
                 The TRIGGER does nothing on the board (no picking up or selecting other creatures,
                 no dice); it clicks menu options. Holding a weapon, the RIGHT trigger uses it when
                 that laser is not on a menu (WEAPONS below).
RADIAL MENUS ... A uses the laser you can see:
                 - No grip squeezed and no radial menu open (lasers hidden): A opens the menu of the
                   creature you are, wherever you happen to point.
                 - A grip squeezed (that hand's laser shows; the right one if both): A on another
                   creature, or on a door or chest, opens its menu (replacing an open one); A
                   anywhere else opens the menu of the creature you are, or closes an open menu.
                 - A radial menu open (both lasers show; A uses the right one unless only the left
                   grip is squeezed): A on another creature / door / chest opens its menu instead;
                   A on the menu itself, the floor or the sky closes it; A on the creature whose
                   menu is open keeps it open.
                 The name of the creature or object whose menu it is is written in the middle of the
                 ring (in TaleSpire's menu font; long names shrink and wrap to fit inside the ring).
                 Every radial menu has an extra teal button above the ring: "Exit First Person"
                 (and unique creatures' menus the blue "Hand Colour" and red "Weapons" buttons next
                 to it).
PREDATOR VISION  (Game Master only.) Hold BOTH trackpads (press them in, like clicking them, and keep
                 holding; on controllers without trackpads, press in both grips and hold them for
                 about half a second): the
                 world goes almost grey and dark, while every creature you can see keeps its full
                 colour and gets a hazy blood-red glow around it - handy to show the players where
                 everyone is. Let go and it fades back. It is not X-ray vision: walls and anything
                 else in front still hide creatures. The stream view shows it too. (In first person
                 the trackpads do nothing else, apart from a quick single tap still pinging.)
HAND COLOURS ... A unique creature can have hands of its own colour while you are it. Open its radial
                 menu (in first person or not, yours or another creature's you control) and pull the
                 trigger on the blue hand button above the ring: an arc of colours opens above the menu -
                 Default (HandColor in TaleSpireVR.FirstPerson.ini), seven skin tones (Porcelain, Fair, Beige,
                 Tan, Bronze, Brown, Deep Brown), Pale Red, Purple, Light Black, Orc Green and Bone
                 White. Point at one (its name shows) and pull the trigger; the creature's current
                 colour has a bright ring. If you are that creature, your hands change at once. The
                 hand button again closes the arc; A anywhere on the menu closes the whole menu.
                 Saved on this computer (BepInEx\config\TaleSpireVR\TaleSpireVR.HandColors.txt, per
                 campaign) and only seen by you: TaleSpire's cloud has no place for it except the
                 creature's name or link, which every player sees. If the creature stops being unique,
                 its colour is forgotten.
WEAPONS ........ A unique creature can carry weapons into first person. On its radial menu (in first
                 person or not; only creatures you control have the button), the red button with a
                 crossed sword and staff ("Weapons") opens an
                 arc above the menu: Unarmed at the left end, then the weapons added to this creature,
                 then + at the right end (more than about ten continue on a second arc outside the
                 first, more than 24 on a third, with slightly smaller buttons). The one it holds has a
                 bright ring. Pull the trigger on one
                 to hold it (Unarmed: nothing in the hand): in your right hand at once if you are that
                 creature, otherwise the next time you become it. New creatures start with none.
  CATALOGUE .... + opens the Weapon Catalogue where the radial menu was: a grid of dark squares, each with
                 a picture of the weapon and its name:
                   guns ...... Shotgun, Combat Shotgun, Pistol, Uzi, Assault Rifle, Machine Gun, Sniper

                   ranged .... Bow, Magic Wand, Fire Staff
                   melee ..... Longsword, Scimitar, Battle Axe, Hand Axe, Fire Axe, War Hammer,
                               Sledgehammer, Morning Star, Spiked Bat, Spear, Halberd, Glaive, Scythe,
                               Dagger, Knife, Machete, Crowbar, Shield, Kite Shield
                   powers .... Flame, Ice
                 Trigger on one adds it to this creature (it gets a
                 green tick); trigger on a ticked one takes it away again. Return (top left) brings back

                 It works the same in first person and at the table. Saved on this computer
                 (BepInEx\config\TaleSpireVR\TaleSpireVR.Weapons.txt, per campaign), like hand colours;
                 forgotten if the creature stops being unique. Only you see the weapons in your hand.
  HOLDING ...... A gun or melee weapon takes the place of your right hand: the hand (and arm) isn't
                 drawn, just the weapon, sitting where your real hand would hold it and moving with it:
                 guns by their pistol grip (the rocket launcher by its trigger grip, the musket by the

                 forward; the magic wand along your hand like a short stick, tip forward; the fire staff
                 upright, its crystal at the top; melee weapons by the handle, head or blade up and
                 forward (the axes' edges and the hammers' faces forward; spears and polearms by the

                 top / tip away from you - so the handle sits at the same angle as the controller's in

                 Unarmed or with a power (Flame, Ice) your right hand is back, fingers tracked as usual.

                 really starts near your hand, so a menu closer than the tip can still be pointed at -
                 you see just its dot there), out of the front of the bow where the arrow would fly,
                 out of the wand's tip and the staff's crystal, out of the middle of the shield. Grips,
                 radial menus and A
                 work as usual, and the right trigger on a menu still clicks it.
  RIGHT TRIGGER  (laser not on a menu) uses the weapon:
    Guns ....... one shot per trigger pull (machine guns too): a quick blast of fire out of that gun's
                 own muzzle with a bang. With the right laser showing (grip squeezed) on another
                 creature, that creature is also attacked with TaleSpire's red attack (Red Laser, as
                 from its radial menu > Attacks) - everyone sees that. The Injector is a sci-fi gun:
                 its attack is the green one (Green Laser). On your screen and the stream the laser
                 leaves at once and strikes about a fifth of a second after the shot: TaleSpire's
                 wait before its laser leaves (almost half a second) is skipped. Other players see
                 TaleSpire's usual attack.
    Bow ........ one arrow per trigger pull: no flash, the string's twang, and with the right laser
                 on another creature TaleSpire's green attack (Green Laser) - quick on your screen,
                 as with the guns.
    Magic Wand . a purple flash at the wand's tip with a magic zap; on a creature, TaleSpire's
                 Magic Missile attack.
    Fire Staff . a burst of fire out of the crystal, the way your hand points, with a whoosh; on a
                 creature, TaleSpire's Magic Missile attack.
    Melee ...... (Longsword, Scimitar, Battle Axe, Hand Axe, Fire Axe, War Hammer, Sledgehammer,
                 Morning Star, Spiked Bat, Spear, Halberd, Glaive, Scythe, Dagger, Knife, Machete,
                 Crowbar, Shield, Kite Shield)
                 hold the trigger and bring the weapon into another creature (a tenth of its blade,
                 shaft or head inside it is enough; the axes and hammers strike with the head as well
                 as the shaft, the shields with their face): it is struck once - TaleSpire's Hit attack (as
                 from its radial menu > Attacks > Hit; everyone sees it) with a hit sound. On your
                 screen and the stream it lands at once: TaleSpire's run-up for it (almost a second) is
                 skipped, and its own swing and thud sounds are left out, so each hit makes one sound.
                 Other players see TaleSpire's usual Hit, landing about a second later.
                 Keeping the weapon in it does nothing more: pull it out of the creature completely and
                 strike again (or strike another creature) for the next hit, at least 0.4 seconds
                 later (a weapon grazing a creature's edge counts once). No laser needed.
    Flame ...... nothing in your hand. Hold the trigger: a bright fiery sigil appears at once in front
                 of your right palm, already about palm-sized, facing the way the palm faces, slowly
                 turning and lighting your hand orange; a line of fire runs along the floor from below

                 grows (most of it early on, to about the length of your hand) and moves a little away

                 Which TaleSpire attack the guns and the melee weapons play: ShotgunAttack / SwordAttack

    WHO ........ Weapons, flame and ice only reach creatures you can see: not ones the Game Master

EXIT ...........Point at "Exit First Person" and pull the trigger. The view blinks back to your

RULER .......... Left stick CLICK (with the stick at rest - a click while walking doesn't count):

                 the campaign's units; the dial turns like a tape measure's spool - clockwise while
                 you move away from your starting point, back the other way as you come back.
                 It starts again from zero each time you enter first person.
ESC MENU ....... B opens TaleSpire's ESC menu (Resume, Settings, Exit to Main Menu, Exit to Desktop)
                 in front of you, all by itself: nothing else of TaleSpire's menus is shown or can be

                 way, and the Main Menu / Desktop buttons ask Yes / No as usual. B again, A, or the

NOT AVAILABLE .. The big menu panel, TaleSpire's toolbars and dice (hidden while you are a creature,

Settings (walking speed, step height, jump height, gravity, eye height, how far you can see, hand
colour, the weapons' attacks) are in TaleSpireVR.FirstPerson.ini. Heights and speeds are in board squares; for
scale, a human mini is about 1.15 squares tall.

Black flickers at the edge of view on very busy boards come from the headset filling in frames when
the game can't keep up, in first person and at the table alike (TaleSpire's own per-frame work, not
the mod's first person). SteamVR's Motion Smoothing setting (on or off) changes how those filled-in
frames look.


HOTKEY BUTTONS (row under the big menu panel, on a board)
---------------------------------------------------------
Point and pull the trigger:
  Menus (Space)  - opens TaleSpire's side menus (creature & asset libraries, etc.)
  Build Mode (B) - toggle Build Mode (lit while on), like the left thumbstick click: it always works
                   (Game Master only: greyed out for players, like Build Grid)
  Undo / Redo    - Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y
  Copy / Paste   - Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V (slabs)
  Delete         - delete the selection
  Shift (hold)   - latches Shift on/off (slide modifier in Build Mode); lit while on.
                   Remember to turn it off again.
  Build Grid     - TaleSpire's build grid on/off (lit while on). It only exists in Build
                   Mode, so this switches Build Mode on first (the same reliable way as the
                   left thumbstick click). The grid is drawn at the build
                   height around your laser; move it with the build pin on the floor slider.
  Cut Box (N)    - TaleSpire's cut box on/off (lit while on)
  Esc            - Escape (close menus / back)
  Reset View     - back to the default zoom, standing at the camera focus point


TIPS
----
- Your real-world steps move you around the board too; use the left stick, right stick
  and zoom buttons to cover big maps from a small play space.
- Your physical keyboard and mouse still work if you need to type (chat, names, search).
- A flashing red "!" in the very top-left corner of the menu is TaleSpire's own error console
  (it lights up when the game logs an error); clicking it opens the console with the details.
- Menu text sharpness: MenuSharpness in TaleSpireVR.Menus.ini (0 = soft, 1 = sharpest).
- Menu size: MenuScale in the same file (1.2 = 20% bigger than the flat game; try 1.3 or 1.4
  for even bigger buttons - bigger menus show a little less at once). SidePanelScale makes the
  left-hand buttons, the top-right buttons (unique creatures, chat), the menus they open, and
  the right-hand floor slider, bigger still
  (1.5 = another 50%). Long menus such as Atmosphere then need a little scrolling.
- Frame rate: zooming in draws less of the board and fewer shadows, so it runs faster.
  On very busy boards, turning off "Reflections" in TaleSpire's graphics settings gains
  a few more fps. The mod's own speed settings (draw distance, shadow distance,
  how many torches cast shadows) are in TaleSpireVR.Graphics.ini.
- All settings are in the .ini files directly in the VRMod folder (SETTINGS FILES at the top of
  this file; edit while the game is closed). The mod never touches TaleSpire's own settings files.
- To play TaleSpire flat, click the VR MODE button on the main menu (see the top of this file).
- You can remap any of these in SteamVR: Settings > Controllers > Manage Controller
  Bindings > TaleSpire. The default binding is "TaleSpire VR - Valve Index controllers
  defaults"; if you made your own binding before, switch back to the default (or add the new
  actions - finger skeletons, trigger / grip pull, thumb rest - to yours).


OTHER CONTROLLERS
-----------------
The mod ships SteamVR default bindings for these controllers too; SteamVR picks the right one by
itself when you start the game. Remap anything in SteamVR's Controller Bindings screen (as above),
like any SteamVR game. Your hands' fingers follow the trigger and grip (only the Index tracks each
finger), and the mod adjusts how hands and weapons sit for each controller type.
  Meta Touch / Pico / Vive Focus 3 / HP Reverb G2 / Vive Cosmos (thumbsticks, no trackpads):
    A / B (right) and X / Y (left) .. as the Index's A / B (Y = zoom in, X = zoom out)
    sticks and stick clicks ........ as on the Index
    grip squeezed all the way ...... the firm grip squeeze (measure / lasso)
    grip pressed (half way) ........ the trackpad press: turn / raise while carrying, both for
                                     a second = stream view hold (GM), quick tap = ping;
                                     both held about half a second in first person = Predator
                                     Vision (GM). On the Vive Cosmos the bumpers do this instead.
  Vive wands (one trackpad, menu button, grip button):
    trackpad touch + slide ......... the sticks (left = move, right = turn / up-down)
    left pad press up / down ....... zoom in / out;   left pad centre press = left trackpad press
    right pad press up / down ...... A (menus) / stick click;  right pad centre = right trackpad press
    menu buttons ................... left = Build Mode, right = B (menu)
    grip buttons ................... measure (left) / lasso (right)
  Windows Mixed Reality (stick + trackpad):
    sticks ......................... as on the Index
    left pad press up / down ....... zoom in / out;   left pad centre press = left trackpad press
    right pad press up ............. A (menus);       right pad centre press = right trackpad press
    menu buttons ................... right = B (menu), left = A
    grip buttons ................... measure (left) / lasso (right)
These were made from each controller's SteamVR layout but only tested on Valve Index controllers.
